  species trait. Fix: "EXACTLY ONE lobster claw, LEFT arm; right hand is an
  ordinary furry paw that holds the pencil." Fixed most panels, not all.

  the model drop the claw entirely. Fix: assert presence before absence —
  "always exactly one, clearly visible" paired with "never two" — and give
  feet a positive replacement (brown lace-ups) rather than only a prohibition.
- Gym drift: round 1 put panel 2 in a dim blue cardio gym and panel 4 back in
  the attic. Fix: THE GYM described once in its own section, both panels
  refer to it by name. Held in one pass.
- Cropped head on a cinematic angle. Fix: "every character stays FULLY IN
  FRAME with head and face never cropped" on any camera direction.
- TECHNOLOGIST garbles on small figures. Unfixed; stage Igor large in at
  least two panels.
