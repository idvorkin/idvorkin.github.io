# Den #21 "Thinner": shot list

Each panel is the END of an action. Panel clips (p1–p4) carry the lines; the
in-betweens (b*) show the action that makes the next panel. The final cut is
the `stitch.sh` line in `../README.md` step 11. Paths (`mid/`, `bridges/`) are
in the run dir; the prompts that made them are in `prompts/`.

## P1 → P2: the paint roll

- **In the picture (P2):** a fat blue diagonal stripe over the house, drips running; Igor's paws are up and his mouth open; Larry has the roller on the board.
- **Action that made it:** Larry lifts the loaded roller to the top-left corner and rolls ONE stroke down to the bottom-right, across the house. Igor's smile collapses.
- **Legibility:** the stroke is diagonal like P2's. Paint appears only behind the roller, and drips start as it leaves.
- **Camera:** P2 is tighter, so the camera moves in during the wind-up.
- **Render:** midpoint `mid/m12.png`, with the stripe half done, stopping at the house's roof. Clips: `b12a` START→MID on Kling (raise plus push-in), `b12b` MID→P2 on Veo (finish the stroke, Igor reacts).

## P2 → P3: Larry exits

- **In the picture (P3):** Larry is gone, his roller is on the floor and the stair hatch is open. Igor is alone, paw at chin.
- **Action that made it:** Larry drops the roller with a clatter, scurries to the hatch and climbs down. Igor watches him go, then turns to the board.
- **Camera:** new framing (closer on Igor, the hatch in view). A cut on the action inside the clip is fine.
- **Render:** midpoint `mid/m23.png`, with Larry halfway down the hatch. `b23a` and `b23b` are both on Kling. The Veo take of `b23b` had Larry climb back up.

## P3 → P4: the thinner

- **In the picture (P4):** the board is bare and dripping, the house is gone too, and a puddle spreads round Igor's crocs. Larry holds out a THINNER tin.
- **Action that made it:** Larry pops back up the hatch with the tin and sloshes it over the board. The stripe AND the house run off, and the puddle spreads. This splash is the punchline "It does both."
- **Camera:** new framing, with the board moving to the center.
- **Render:** midpoint `mid/m34.png` (mid-pour, half dissolved). `b34a` and `b34b` are both on Kling. The first `b34a` take gave Larry dark glasses and was re-run. Veo gave him two claws.
