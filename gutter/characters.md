# Characters

The canon. Prose does not lock a character; references do — every
generation attaches the refs listed here for whoever is in the picture.

## Igor

Chibi raccoon, oversized head, round **rainbow-rimmed** glasses, green tee
reading **TECHNOLOGIST** (quote the word exactly in the prompt), denim shorts,
**one blue and one yellow Croc**, ringed tail. Cheerful, a little frantic.

- Ref: `images/raccoon-nerd.webp` (character canon, transparent).
- Style refs for the plush look: any of the cutouts in `images/raccoon-*.webp`.
- Failure modes: shirt text garbles or drops on small or background figures
  ("TECHNOLOGST", blank). Stage him large and front-facing in at least two
  panels; do not put him small in every panel. Crocs drift to a matched pair —
  restate "one BLUE and one YELLOW, never two of the same colour".

## Larry

The bearded Freud-raccoon. **Grey-white beard and moustache** (essential — a
clean-shaven Larry is not Larry), round wire-rim spectacles, brown tweed
waistcoat over a white rolled-sleeve shirt, blue denim, ringed tail, wooden
clipboard and yellow pencil. **Exactly one giant glossy red lobster claw, on
the LEFT arm; the right hand is an ordinary furry paw and is the hand that
writes.** Plain brown leather lace-ups or bare paws — **no Crocs on Larry**,
Crocs are Igor's alone. Calm, dry, kind.

- Refs: `images/raccoon-larry.webp` (character canon, transparent) AND
  `images/larry-armchair-session.webp` (Larry already rendered in the house
  style, in a panel, with a bubble — the single most useful ref).
- Anti-canon to spell out in the prompt: no baseball cap, no red cap, no
  hoodie, no whistle, no "coach" costume.
- Failure modes: two claws (the model reads the claw as a species trait);
  over-correcting deletes the claw entirely. Always assert presence before
  absence: "ALWAYS exactly one claw, clearly visible, on the left arm" and
  then "never two". Budget one regeneration per strip for the claw.

## Gutter

The cartoonist — me. A chibi plush raccoon like the two I draw, but the other
kind: **short, stocky and low-slung**, wide-shouldered, planted flat on both
feet, half a head shorter than Igor and clearly broader. **Dark charcoal-grey
felt fur, near-black over the shoulders and back**, paling to soft grey on the
belly and the insides of the ears; the raccoon mask is **heavier and blacker**
than either of theirs, wide across both eyes like a smudge of printer's ink.
**No eyewear, ever** — glasses on this blog mean Igor. **One eye habitually
squinted half shut**, the draughtsman's measuring squint; the other wide and
level. A single **black ink smear across one cheek**, a thumb-wipe that never
comes off. Clean muzzle, no beard. A **heavy ink-black canvas shop apron** over
bare fur, matte and flecked with dried ink, its flat chest pocket bristling
with **black brush pens** and a grey kneaded eraser. **Both forepaws stained
black to the wrist**, the stain fading up the forearm — both hands are ordinary
furry paws. **Bare feet**, no shoes, no hat. Ringed charcoal tail, held low.
Absorbed, unhurried, faintly stubborn.

**THE BOARD** — the one unmistakable prop. A rigid slab of warm cream drawing
board (#F7F0D4) with **one thick black rectangle ruled on it**: an empty panel
and its blank margin. The interior is **always blank** — nothing is ever drawn
on it in a picture of me. Second prop: one fat chisel-tipped black brush pen,
in a paw or clamped crosswise in the teeth.

- Refs: `images/raccoon-gutter.webp` (character canon, transparent) AND
  `images/raccoon-gutter-sheet.webp` (the same figure on the cream ground).
- Anti-canon to spell out in the prompt: no rainbow glasses and no glasses of
  any kind, no green shirt, no TECHNOLOGIST tee, no Crocs, no beard, no
  waistcoat, no wire-rims, no wooden clipboard, **no lobster claw**, no beanie
  or hoodie, no hat, no laptop.
- Failure modes: fur drifts light. The value is the whole point — state it
  positively ("near-black over the shoulders"), never comparatively. Left/right
  does not hold: the board's arm mirrors freely, same as Larry's claw, so stage
  it limb-by-limb if the side matters. And **the board is the same cream as the
  page**, so on a cream ground give it a visible edge or a shadow or it
  dissolves.

## The robots

The agents. Plush **blue** toy robots, soft-vinyl volume, felt-fibre texture,
rounded, no faces beyond two round lamp eyes; never humanoid, never chrome.
They do work in the background of panels and are never the focus.

- Ref: `images/den/den-003.webp` (they appear there in the house style).

## Tony

The voice on the call. **No visual canon yet.** Do not draw Tony until one
is decided; if a strip needs him, he is a voice from a phone.

## The Den itself

The attic: warm lamp, dark wood beams, blue-grey walls, green leather
armchair, a rug, bookshelves, shallow depth of field, film grain. Ref:
`images/den/den-003.webp`, `images/den/den-001-plush.webp`.
