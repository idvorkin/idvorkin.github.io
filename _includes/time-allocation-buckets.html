{% comment %} Time-allocation buckets — five bucket rows for a 14 hour day to spend (24h minus 8 sleep minus about 2 for meals and transit), drawn as one day with arrows between the buckets. Controls: a Weekday / Weekend flip (two made-up baselines, each with its own relationships ceiling), a No plan / Planned switch (Planned is the target allocation), two sliders for work and my own tech, and a "push the hour up / let it slide back" stepper that walks one hour up the ladder consumption -> my own tech -> work. Tech as a whole is either below its baseline or above it, never both: below, the freed hours flow by the plan's shares with ceilings (relationships "their time", health "enough"; anything past a ceiling runs into consumption; with no plan most of it goes straight to consumption); above, the extra hours are taken from consumption first, then identity, relationships and health. The day always adds up to 14 hours and a stacked day strip shows the total. The Tech bar is split into work and my own. The arrows live in a clipped gutter left of the rows, one tree per move (a spine plus a stub into each row it touches), thin uniform strokes, never over text. Every bucket name and the Point the hour control carry a "?" that opens a short pop-up (tap, click or keyboard; Escape or an outside tap closes it). Ceiling notes, "counts for" readouts, day strip, summary line and arrows update in one render. Self-contained, no parameters, no libraries, no network. Edit the DATA block at the top of the script. THE HOURS ARE ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT MEASURED: plausible round numbers, not a log of anyone's week. Used by: _d/time-allocation.md {% endcomment %}
