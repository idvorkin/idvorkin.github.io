What 12 hours count for 27h

Phone hours count for nothing. Tech counts for itself. Work counts for four, because I'm aligned and a company carries it. Move an hour and see.

The multiplier is made up, like the hours. It stands for being aligned with a company that reviews, ships and runs what I build. Lose the alignment and work drops back to counting for one.
