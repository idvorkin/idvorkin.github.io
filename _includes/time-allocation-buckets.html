{% comment %} Time-allocation buckets — five bucket rows for a 16 hour waking day, a "hours out of tech" slider, and a No plan / Planned switch. Planned hours go to relationships, identity and health until relationships hits "their time" and health hits "enough"; anything past a ceiling runs into consumption. With no plan, most of the freed hours go straight to consumption. Self-contained, no parameters, no libraries, no network. Edit the DATA block at the top of the script; the rows, the ceiling ticks and the leak line are all generated from it. THE HOURS ARE ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT MEASURED: plausible round numbers for a 16 hour waking day, not a log of anyone's week. Used by: _d/time-allocation.md {% endcomment %}

Take hours out of tech

Sixteen waking hours, made up to show the shape. Move the slider, then switch between an hour with no plan and an hour with one.

    The dashed tick is a ceiling. Relationships stops at whatever time the other people actually have. Health stops at enough. Planned hours that run past a ceiling do not disappear, they land in consumption. Hours with no plan mostly go there directly. Numbers are made up, not measured.
