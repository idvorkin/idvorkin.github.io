{% comment %} Time-allocation buckets — five bucket rows for a 16 hour waking day drawn as one day with arrows between the buckets. Two moves: a "hours out of tech" slider with a No plan / Planned switch (Planned is the target allocation: freed hours go to relationships, identity and health until relationships hits "their time" and health hits "enough", and anything past a ceiling runs into consumption; with no plan most of it goes straight to consumption), and a "push the hour up / let it slide back" stepper that walks one hour up the ladder consumption -> my own tech -> work. The Tech bar is split into work and my own. The arrows in the left gutter are an SVG drawn from the computed flows every render; the ceiling notes, the "counts for" readouts and the summary line update in the same pass. Self-contained, no parameters, no libraries, no network. Edit the DATA block at the top of the script. THE HOURS ARE ILLUSTRATIVE, NOT MEASURED: plausible round numbers for a 16 hour waking day, not a log of anyone's week. Used by: _d/time-allocation.md {% endcomment %}

Move an hour

Sixteen waking hours, made up to show the shape. Take hours out of tech and watch where they land; Planned is my target. Then push one hour up the screen, from the phone into my own tech into work, or let it slide back.

Point the hour

    The arrows show where the last move went. The dashed tick is a ceiling: Relationships stops at whatever time the other people actually have, Health stops at enough. Planned hours that run past a ceiling do not disappear, they land in consumption, and hours with no plan mostly go there directly. Tech is two segments, work and my own, because the same screen hour counts for very different amounts depending on where it points. Numbers, and how much an hour counts for, are made up, not measured.
