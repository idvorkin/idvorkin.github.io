{%- comment -%} Side-view backpack model for /back-pain, the companion to banana-back.html. It reuses that figure's .bb styles and spine chain, so include it AFTER banana-back.html on the same page. The model is one torque balance about the hip: the trunk (pelvis + spine + head) leans forward until the upper body's weight in front of the hip cancels the pack's weight behind it. tan(lean) = m*d / (M*h + m*y), where m is the part of the pack the shoulders carry, d how far behind the hip its center of mass hangs and y how high. Latching the straps pulls the pack up and in (smaller d) and a hip belt hands up to ~30% of the vertical load to the pelvis (Lafiandra & Harman 2004), so less of m is on the trunk. The eyes stay level: only the top of the neck (C0-C2) extends to undo the lean, so the head rides forward with the trunk. Self-contained: inline SVG + vanilla JS, one instance per page. {%- endcomment -%}
