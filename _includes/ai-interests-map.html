{% comment %} Interactive AI interests map, transcribed from a hand-drawn mind map. Three branches (Training, Usage, Inference), each with up to two levels of children, laid out as columns that stack on a phone. Tapping a node (or a topic chip in the header) opens its card below the map. Cards are rendered server-side in .aim-cards; without JavaScript they show as a plain list under the tree, with JavaScript that list hides and the clicked card is copied into the panel. Data: _data/ai_interests_map.yml. Self-contained, no libraries, no network. Used by: _d/ai-interests-map.md {% endcomment %} {%- assign aim = site.data.ai_interests_map -%}
