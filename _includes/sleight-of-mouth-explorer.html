{% comment %} Sleight of Mouth explorer (interactive). Pick a limiting belief from the dropdown; it shows as Hall and Bodenhamer's equation (external behavior -> link -> internal state, color-coded like the belief builder in sleight-of-mouth-equation.html; the data keys stay evidence / link / conclusion). Nothing is selected to start: a "pick a pattern" prompt sits where the reframe goes. Click any of Dilts' fourteen patterns: the part of the belief it aims at lights up, the chip shows a one-line hint of what the move does, the reframed sentence appears directly under the belief, and the panel shows the pattern's question. "About this pattern" in the pattern card opens a short explanation (click/tap toggles; Esc or a tap outside closes). Prev / Next and the arrow keys step through all fourteen. An "All 14 | By section | By direction" toggle switches to cards for the post's own sections (sections: in the data) or for Hall and Bodenhamer's seven Mind-Lines directions (directions: in the data); tapping a card runs the same explorer on just that card's patterns, with "All sections" / "All directions" to go back. Without JavaScript it falls back to a plain list of the patterns. Data: _data/sleight_of_mouth.yml. Self-contained, no libraries, no network. Used by: _d/2016-3-12-sleight-of-mouth.md {% endcomment %}
