{% comment %} Sleight of Mouth explorer (interactive). Pick a limiting belief from the dropdown, then click any of Dilts' fourteen patterns: the part of the belief it aims at lights up (evidence, link, conclusion, or the whole belief), the reframed sentence appears directly under the belief, and the panel shows the pattern's question. The "?" beside each pattern opens a short explanation (click/tap toggles; Esc or a tap outside closes). Prev / Next and the arrow keys step through all fourteen. An "All 14 | By section" toggle switches to cards for the post's own sections (sections: in the data); tapping one runs the same explorer on just that section's patterns, with "All sections" to go back. Without JavaScript it falls back to a plain list of the patterns. Data: _data/sleight_of_mouth.yml. Self-contained, no libraries, no network. Used by: _d/2016-3-12-sleight-of-mouth.md {% endcomment %}
