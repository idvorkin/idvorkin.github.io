{% comment %} Sleight of Mouth explorer (interactive). Pick a limiting belief, then click any of Dilts' fourteen patterns: the part of the belief it aims at lights up (evidence, link, conclusion, or the whole belief), and the panel shows the pattern's question plus the reframed sentence for that belief. Prev / Next and the arrow keys step through all fourteen. Without JavaScript it falls back to a plain list of the patterns. Data: _data/sleight_of_mouth.yml. Self-contained, no libraries, no network. Used by: _d/2016-3-12-sleight-of-mouth.md {% endcomment %}
