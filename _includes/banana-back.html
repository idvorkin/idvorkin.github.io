The spine is a chain of vertebrae (L5 up to C1) sitting on the sacrum, and the whole point is the compensation chain: - Hip strength tilts the pelvis forward about the hip joint, which steepens the sacral slope. A rigid spine would tip with it (grey ghost, gaze dropping to the floor). The eyes stay level instead, so lumbar lordosis grows by the same angle, weighted about 2/3 into L4-S1 (the lower arc of lordosis tracks sacral slope: Roussouly; Chevillotte 2022). - Head forward flexes the lower neck (C4-C7) and extends the upper neck (C0-C2) by the same total, so the head translates forward but stays level (Khayatzadeh 2017).
