{% comment %} Map of Hall and Bodenhamer's seven directions (interactive). The belief sits in the center inside a dashed "box"; content reframing sits inside the box and the other six directions radiate out (outframing up, deframing down, pre-framing back/left, post-framing forward/right, counter reframing back on itself, analogous framing sideways). Tapping a direction opens a panel with its move, Dilts' patterns, the book's mind-lines and the "Skipped the gym" example, plus a button that opens the explorer (_includes/sleight-of-mouth-explorer.html) on that direction via its somxShow hand-off, and a link to the post's section. Data: directions in _data/sleight_of_mouth.yml (heading, idea, patterns, lines, example, anchor, color); the angles live here. Without JavaScript it falls back to a list. Self-contained, no libraries, no network. Used by: _d/2016-3-12-sleight-of-mouth.md {% endcomment %}
