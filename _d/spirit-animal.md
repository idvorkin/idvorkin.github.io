## Wet paws, busy paws

A raccoon understands the world through its paws: its brain spends more of its sensing power on touch than almost any other animal's. My paws have two jobs. In the physical world they make smiles and wonder: cards that change color on command, coins passing through a spectator's hand, [balloons](/balloon), and back in my 30s, juggling fire. That's my [Dealer of Smiles and Wonder](/eulogy#dealer-of-smiles-and-wonder) role. In the virtual world they build: code, tools, and these days a lot of tinkering with AI.

And raccoons washing their food is an old wives' tale. They're not cleaning anything: wetting their paws [sharpens their sense of touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raccoon#Dousing) before they handle their dinner. I'm not big on hand washing either, so I respect that. I wet my paws every morning before I touch the day: 5am, a [gratitude journal](/process-journal), then 20 minutes of meditation. That's my [Emotionally Healthy Human](/eulogy#emotionally-healthy-human) role.

## No latch left unopened

To a raccoon, every latch is an invitation: it fiddles until the thing opens, then remembers how next time. That's the tinkerer in me, my [Technologist](/eulogy#technologist) role: vim, tmux, a dotfile collection, a vim plugin for OneNote, and more [pet projects](/pet-projects) than I'll admit to. Puzzles that would annoy other people are the fun part for me.

The raccoon also fails without drama: try, fail, try again. My discipline starts strong and falls off the wagon, so that example matters. I [restart the next day](/affirmations#do-it-anyways-deliberate-disciplined-daily) without beating myself up.

And sometimes a raccoon skips the puzzle and tips the whole thing over. That's how I like to explore. My [AI journal](/ai-journal#ai-helps-me-think-by-showing-me-options) is mostly me trying everything, having AI show me a field of options, and picking by eye.

## Mischief in a mask

The raccoon walks around looking like it's up to something, and gets away with it. I'd rather be a little ridiculous than a little boring: a silly hat, an $8 TEMU crazy shirt, and a [Tesla named Tony](/tesla) that makes fart sounds every time I get a text. The mascot wears mismatched Crocs for a reason.

## City slicker, garbage gourmet

The raccoon loves the city and makes a meal out of whatever's around. I'm a city person and a generalist. As a [Mostly Car-Free Spirit](/eulogy#mostly-car-free-spirit), that means walking the kids to school, the subway plus a folding [bike](/bike), and bus-to-the-airport-elevator adventures that cost nothing. As a generalist, it means magic, balloons, juggling, kettlebells, bikes, code and writing. No single specialty, a lot of roles.

## When the raccoon falls short…

One raccoon flaw I share: raccoons raid the trash for easy calories, and my version is short-form video. When real work feels expensive, I reach for a [TikTok thought escape](/addiction#tiktok-thought-escape). Fair cop.

The rest of the raccoon's resume, I'll pass on:

- **Up all night.** I'm up at 5am. A raccoon who keeps lark hours.
- **Nuisance.** I'd rather leave strangers with a smile than a mess.
- **Absent dad.** Raccoon fathers take no part in raising their kits. That's the opposite of the [Family Man](/eulogy#family-man) I'm trying to be.

That last gap is the big one, and it's where the crow comes in.

## …the crow steps up

The crow doesn't make a cute mascot, but everywhere the raccoon fails me, especially family and community, the crow is what I aspire to.

- **Family helpers.** Young crows stay home for years to help raise their little siblings. When Zach lets a scared Amelia sleep in his bed, that's a crow family. I want to give that kind of [complete attention](/eulogy#family-man).
- **Partners for the long haul.** Crow couples keep the same home together year after year. That's [Tori](/eulogy#husband-to-tori---his-life-long-partner), and I'll keep it Tori-light, as promised.
- **Never forgets a face.** Wrong a crow and its whole neighborhood will know your face. I want to remember people that well, and the [Relationship Coach](/mortality-software#relationship-coach) in my mortality software exists because I don't do it naturally.
- **A flock, not a lone forager.** Crows gather by the thousands to roost together at night. My flock runs on rhythm: friends I see every week, friends I deliberately make time for every month, and the mentors I keep going back to. Ammon has been my weight-pledge partner for over 30 years ([Fit Fellow](/eulogy#fit-fellow)).
- **Gathers for its dead.** Crows gather around a dead crow to learn what killed it. Learning from death to live better is the whole point of writing my own eulogy and building [mortality software](/mortality-software).
- **Tool maker.** The raccoon picks the lock. The crow builds the pick. I want to be the builder: dotfiles, vim plugins, and [Larry](/larry), my AI life coach.
- **Talks, and means it.** A crow's caws tell the flock how much danger it's in. Teaching out loud, through my [7 Habits video series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_1J7GM3GLzw&list=PLJveOxX-mxxCl4YDfHMyNzMmWUMFxgC1n) and this blog, is my version.

## Trash panda, crow heart

So the raccoon stays. He's the best of how I already am: hands first, curious, a little mischievous, at home in the city, good at a lot of things and great at none, and yes, sometimes digging through the trash for an easy snack. The crow is who I'm trying to grow into: the one who stays for the family, remembers the faces, shows up for the flock and learns from death.

Put that on my [eulogy](/eulogy): a raccoon who aspired to crow virtues.
