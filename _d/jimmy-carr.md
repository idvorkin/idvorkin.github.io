## The books

**Before & Laughter** (2021) is his self-help book: a memoir of feeling stuck in his mid-twenties and how he changed course, written as a manual for changing your own life, with jokes throughout. The publisher files it under self-help and personal development.

{% include amazon.html asin="1529413117" %}

**On Jokes (or, why everything you know about comedy is wrong)** (October 2026, with Amanda Baker and Abi Grant) is the craft book: families of jokes, the building blocks, and comic timing. It belongs next to the joke-structure notes on [How to Make It Funny](/comedy#how-a-joke-is-built). ([Hachette](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/jimmy-carr/on-jokes-or-why-everything-you-know-about-comedy-is-wrong/9781529454246/))

## Clips

- [How do you come up with your jokes?](https://x.com/jimmycarr/status/2124231463499350195) (November 2026, also [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wkAqdONshJY)). A 15-year-old asks him from the audience, and he answers straight: being interesting comes from being interested, hard work beats talent, and school is really training your attention, which you'll need for everything, including love.
