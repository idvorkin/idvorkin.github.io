Stoicism is where I get the one thing [religion](/religion) gives believers for free: permission to stop carrying outcomes I don't control. A believer says "trust in God's plan." A Stoic sorts everything into what's up to me and what isn't, and spends effort only on the first pile. Same relief, no God required.

{% include ai-slop.html percent="50" %}

## The three ideas I borrow

- **Dichotomy of control**: my actions, effort and judgments are mine; outcomes, other people and the weather are not. My version: maximize influence, don't pretend to control. This is the acceptance leg of [spiritual health](/spiritual-health).
- **Amor fati**: love your fate. Not tolerate what happened, want it, because it's the material you get to work with.
- **Memento mori**: remember you will die. A deadline sorts priorities faster than any framework, which is why I wrote my [eulogy](/eulogy) and keep notes on [dying](/death).
