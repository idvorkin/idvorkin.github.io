The Den is my weekly four-panel strip about running an AI staff — a raccoon, the agents it orchestrates, and the attic they keep setting on fire. The format is stolen, with admiration, from Steve Yegge's [The Wheelhouse](https://yegge.ai/comics/); [here's what I stole and why](/ai-yegge-comics).

{% include ai-slop.html percent="70" %}

Sure, this isn't funny yet — but you gotta start somewhere.

One rule, and it's Steve's: every strip lampoons something that actually happened, and every strip links to the receipts. If I can't produce the transcript, I don't get to draw the strip.
