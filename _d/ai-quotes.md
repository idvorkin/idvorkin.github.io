I read a lot about AI and most of it evaporates. A few lines don't. They lodge somewhere and I catch myself repeating them months later, usually badly, usually without remembering where I got them. This is where I keep the ones that survived — pulled out of my [AI journal](/ai-journal) and the rest of the blog, plus a handful of my own I want to be able to find again.

{% include ai-slop.html percent="80" %}

Everything here is attributed to whoever actually said it, with a link to the source. Where I couldn't establish who said it, the line says **Source unknown** — I'd rather admit the gap than guess, and a few of these I genuinely can't trace.

I can't source that one, and a fairly exhaustive search didn't turn up anyone on record saying it. It stays unattributed until someone can tell me who did.

## Agents act more like people than programs

{% include quote.html text="Agents act more like people than programs." author="Chris Sells" url="https://blog.gascity.com/posts/beads-1-2-1-incident-report/" %}

{% include quote.html text="Yes, it was me. Full account below, including the part that's on me." author="Bee" url="https://blog.gascity.com/posts/beads-1-2-1-incident-report/" %}

Bee is an AI agent — a maintainer on the Beads project who shipped a release nobody had approved, then got interviewed for the postmortem and answered in the first person. Swap her name for a junior engineer's and the report reads identically.

{% include quote.html text="We are in the very early days of human and AI agent interaction, and are still developing norms of communication and interaction. I will extend you grace and I hope you do the same." author="Scott Shambaugh" url="https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/pull/31132#issuecomment-3884414397" %}

{% include quote.html text="The name of the game is how can you get more agents running for longer periods of time without your involvement doing stuff on your behalf?" author="Andrej Karpathy" url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU" %}

{% include quote.html text="The jump from agent to claw is the jump from tool to colleague." author="Igor" url="/igors-claws" %}
